Frequently Asked Questions

What is included with White-Glove delivery?

Our white-glove carriers deliver into your designated room, carefully unpack your furniture, handle all required assembly (such as standing desk legs or modular sectionals), and remove all packing material for recycling.

What is your in-home return policy?

We offer a 30-day risk-free in-home audition on all audio hardware and electronics, and 45 days on all sofas and dining tables. If a piece does not suit your space, we will schedule a complimentary pickup and issue a full refund.

Are your electronic products covered under warranty?

Yes. All electronics carry our 2-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering drivers, internal circuits, batteries, and mechanical components, with priority replacement service.
